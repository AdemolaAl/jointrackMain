Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy sets the rules for using Joinvoo, which is operated by Zedapex. It is part of our Terms of Service. Joinvoo is built for serious advertisers; these rules protect them, the people who join their channels, and the platforms we all depend on.

1. You must not use Joinvoo to

  • promote scams, fraud, fake giveaways, phishing, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, or investment offers that promise guaranteed or unrealistic returns;
  • promote anything illegal where you or your audience are located, including unlicensed gambling, financial or medical services where a licence is required;
  • promote counterfeit goods, stolen data, malware, hacking services, weapons, illegal drugs, or content that sexualises minors;
  • harass, threaten, discriminate against or exploit anyone;
  • impersonate a person, brand or company, or mislead people about who you are;
  • send spam, or collect or sell personal data without a lawful basis.

2. Accounts and traffic

  • Do not create fake or duplicate accounts to collect free joins, credits or referral earnings.
  • Do not generate fake clicks, fake joins, bot traffic or fake postbacks, or report deposits that did not happen.
  • Do not use another person’s bot, channel, ad account, pixel or access token without permission.

3. Platform rules

You must follow the Telegram Terms of Service and the advertising policies of every platform you use, including Meta, TikTok and Snapchat. Joinvoo does not change what those platforms allow. If a platform rejects your ads or restricts your account, that is between you and the platform.

4. Protecting the Service

  • Do not try to break into, overload, scrape or disrupt the Service, other accounts, or our providers.
  • Do not probe or test our security without written permission. If you find a vulnerability, report it to us privately.
  • Do not resell or white-label the Service without a written agreement with Zedapex.

5. Your audience

You are responsible for telling the people who join your channels how their data is used where the law requires it, and for honouring their requests. Do not target children.

6. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches. Depending on how serious they are, we may warn you, remove a connection, pause tracking, withhold referral earnings, or suspend or close the account, and report illegal activity to the authorities. Where reasonable, we will tell you first.
