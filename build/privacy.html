Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zedapex (“we”, “us”), the company that operates Joinvoo, collects, uses and protects personal data. It covers two groups of people: customers (people and businesses who use the Joinvoo website and dashboard) and visitors (people who click a customer’s tracking link and join their Telegram channel, group or bot).

1. Our role

  • For customer data (accounts, billing, support), Zedapex is the controller.
  • For visitor data, the customer decides why and how it is used; Zedapex processes it on the customer’s behalf as a processor. Customers are responsible for having a lawful basis and for informing their audience where the law requires.

2. Data we collect about customers

  • Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and the answers you give at sign-up (for example, which platforms you advertise on).
  • Connections: Telegram bot tokens, the address of your own bot server if you use one, and the Meta, TikTok and Snapchat pixel IDs and access tokens you enter.
  • Billing: wallet top-ups, charges, payment references, and crypto or bank transaction references. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s page and never reach our servers.
  • Referrals: who referred you, who you referred, earnings, and the wallet addresses you give for withdrawals.
  • Campaign data you add: ad spend you enter or import (date, platform, campaign, amount), and the affiliate programs you connect.
  • Alerts: if you connect Telegram alerts, the Telegram chat ID our alert bot uses to message you, and your alert preferences.
  • Support: messages you send through the support chat, and the email address you give so we can reply.
  • Technical: log-in times, IP address, browser, and security logs used to keep the Service safe.

3. Data we process about visitors

The click. When someone opens a customer’s tracking link, the Service records the time, IP address, browser (user agent), approximate country, the page address including campaign tags (UTM parameters), and ad click identifiers and cookies set by Meta (fbclid, _fbc, _fbp), TikTok (ttclid, _ttp) or Snapchat (ScCid, _scid). The tracking or join page may load the customer’s own Meta, TikTok or Snapchat pixel.

The join. When that person joins the customer’s Telegram channel or group, or presses Start on their bot, Telegram tells the customer’s bot their Telegram user ID, first and last name, username and language. The Service links this to the click. If they later leave the channel or block the bot, that is recorded too.

Conversions. If the customer sets up a postback, their affiliate program, broker, casino, shop, CRM or bot may tell us that the person became a lead, signed up, deposited, bought something, qualified, or that a deposit was reversed, with the amount, currency and a transaction reference. We match this to the join.

Join requests. If the customer uses join-request mode, the customer’s bot approves the request and may send the person one welcome message with a personal link that includes their Telegram ID, so the customer’s partner can report deposits back.

Click quality. To filter fake traffic, the Service marks clicks that look automated (for example known bot browsers, data-centre IP ranges, or many clicks from one IP in a short time). These flags are used only for the customer’s reports.

What goes to ad platforms. For customers who connect Meta, TikTok or Snapchat, the Service sends conversion events (such as a join, registration or deposit with its value) together with the click identifiers, IP address, browser, and a hashed (scrambled, one-way) Telegram ID. The raw Telegram ID, name and username are never sent to ad platforms.

4. How we use data

  • To provide the Service: tracking joins, showing reports, and sending the events customers ask us to send.
  • To bill customers, pay referral earnings, and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To provide support and send service emails (for example, email confirmation, password resets, payment receipts and important changes).
  • To improve reliability and performance, using aggregated or technical data.

Our legal bases are performance of our contract with customers, our legitimate interests in running and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where it is required. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use visitor data for our own advertising.

5. Who we share data with

  • Telegram, to operate customers’ bots and invite links.
  • Meta, TikTok and Snapchat, only for customers who connect them, as described above.
  • Payment providers, to process top-ups.
  • Service providers who host our servers, store data, and deliver email, under contracts that protect it.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, where the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is reorganised or sold, under the same protections.

6. How long we keep data

  • Clicks that never lead to a join are deleted after 90 days.
  • Join, deposit and statistics records are kept while the customer’s account is open so they can see their history. Customers can delete them or ask us to.
  • Support conversations are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted or anonymised.
  • Billing and payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law require.
  • When an account is closed, its data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it by law.

7. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, access to production systems is restricted, and visitor IDs sent to ad platforms are hashed. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in different countries. Where data moves across borders, we use providers and safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) appropriate under applicable law.

9. Your rights

Under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where it applies, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us. Visitors should contact the channel owner first, as they control their data; if you contact us, we will pass your request on and help them respond. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy.

11. Children

The Service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children for our own purposes. Customers must not target children with tracked campaigns.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell customers by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.
