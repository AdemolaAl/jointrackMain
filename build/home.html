FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to write any code?

No. You make a bot in Telegram, paste a token, and put your Joinvoo link in your ads. Most people are live in about 10 minutes.

How are deposits matched to my ads?

Your broker, casino, affiliate network or bot sends a small postback when someone deposits. Joinvoo matches it to the person who joined and the ad they came from, and sends Purchase with the value to your ad platform. You can also mark a deposit by hand.

Can I keep my own bot, landing page and domain?

Yes. Track your own bot’s Start without sharing its token, keep your landing page with one line of code, and run links on your own domain. Request-to-join channels are supported.

Is this allowed by Telegram?

Yes. Joinvoo only uses Telegram’s official Bot API, the same invite-link features any channel admin can use. Your bot never posts in your channel.

What happens when my credits run out?

Your ads keep working and people still get into your channel. Tracking pauses until you top up, and you’ll see a banner in your dashboard.
