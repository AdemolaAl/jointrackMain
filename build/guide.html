Make a bot with BotFather

Joinvoo uses your own bot. The bot creates a fresh invite link for every person who clicks your ad, so we know exactly which ad each person came from. BotFather is Telegram’s official tool for making bots, and it’s free.

  1. In Telegram, search for @BotFather (with the blue tick) and tap Start.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. Send a name for your bot, for example Daily Deals Tracker. Members never see it.
  4. Send a username that ends in bot, for example dailydeals_track_bot. If it’s taken, try another.
  5. BotFather replies with a long token that looks like 7712045533:AAHk3v…. Tap it to copy it.
Keep the token private. Anyone with it can control your bot. If it leaks, send /revoke to BotFather and paste the new one into Joinvoo.
Use a new bot just for Joinvoo. A bot can only send its messages to one place, so a bot that already runs something else (a shop bot, a support bot, another tracker) can stop working if you connect it. Tracking your bot’s subscribers is the exception; see step 4.

Connect Meta, TikTok or Snapchat

This is what lets your ad platforms see your real joins, so they can find more people like them. Connect one, two or all three. Each join is then sent to every platform you connect.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

You need two things from Events Manager:

  1. Open Events Manager and choose your dataset (pixel) on the left. If you don’t have one, tap Connect data → Web to create it.
  2. Copy the Dataset ID (also called Pixel ID). It’s a long number like 884210395527140.
  3. Open the Settings tab, scroll to Conversions API, and tap Generate access token. Copy the long token.
  4. In Joinvoo, paste both and tap Save and send a test.
To watch the test arrive, open the Test events tab in Events Manager, copy the code shown there (like TEST12345), and paste it into Joinvoo’s Test code box before sending. Clear the test code when you go live, or real joins only show up in the test tab.

TikTok

In TikTok Ads Manager go to Tools → Events → Web events, open your pixel, copy the Pixel code (letters and numbers, like CABC123DEF456GHI), then in its Settings tap Generate access token. Paste both into Joinvoo’s TikTok tab and send a test.

Snapchat

  1. In Snapchat Ads Manager, open Events Manager and select your Snap Pixel (create one if you don’t have it). Copy the Pixel ID. It looks like a1b2c3d4-5678-90ab-cdef-1234567890ab.
  2. Generate a Conversions API token. You’ll find it in Business settings or in the pixel’s setup in Events Manager. Copy it.
  3. Paste both into Joinvoo’s Snapchat tab and tap Send test. The test goes to Snapchat’s validation endpoint, so it checks your ID and token without adding a real event.

Meta, TikTok and Snapchat rename menus now and then. If a name looks different, look for the same words nearby.

Put your tracking link in your ads

On the Channels page, copy your tracking link. It looks like https://joinvoo.com/c/ab12c. Use it everywhere your ad would normally use your Telegram link.

In Meta Ads Manager

  1. Create a campaign with the Leads or Sales objective and choose Website as the conversion location.
  2. At the ad set, pick your dataset (pixel) and the conversion event Subscribe. That’s what Joinvoo sends by default; you can change it to Lead or CompleteRegistration on the Channels page.
  3. At the ad, paste your tracking link as the Website URL.
  4. Under URL parameters, paste this. Each person’s page then shows which campaign and ad brought them:
utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}

In TikTok Ads Manager

Choose a Website conversions objective, pick your pixel and the Subscribe event, and paste the tracking link as the Destination URL.

In Snapchat Ads Manager

Choose a Website conversions goal, pick your Snap Pixel and the Subscribe event, and paste the tracking link as the ad’s Web URL (the website attachment).

If an ad platform’s review is strict with your ads, set When someone taps your ad to Join page on the Channels page. Visitors then see a clean page with a Join button instead of being sent straight to Telegram.
Don’t share the tracking link inside Telegram itself, and don’t replace it with your channel’s normal invite link. Only clicks that go through the tracking link can be matched to an ad.

Track deposits and sales

Deposit tracking is part of Pro. Every account gets a free trial: 7 days or 20 FTDs, whichever comes first (see step 12).

If your channel or bot sends people to a broker, casino, shop or app, you can send their sign-ups, first deposits, repeat deposits and sales back to your ad platforms too. Your ads then learn to find depositors, not just joiners. These events cost nothing extra.

  1. In your dashboard, open Conversions and copy your postback URL. It’s unique to your account and looks like this:
https://joinvoo.com/pb/<your-key>?sub1={telegram_id}&status=ftd&payout={payout}&currency=USD&txid={transaction_id}
  1. Pass the Telegram ID to your offer. Your bot knows the Telegram ID of everyone who presses Start. Add it to the offer link the bot sends as sub1, for example https://offer.example.com/?sub1=7012345678. Most networks and trackers call this a sub ID or click ID.
  2. Paste the postback URL into your affiliate network, Keitaro, Binom, CRM or broker as the postback for registrations and deposits. Swap the placeholders for that tool’s own macros: its sub1 macro for {telegram_id}, its payout or amount macro for {payout}, and its transaction ID macro for {transaction_id}.
  3. Set status to the event: lead, reg (sign-up), ftd (first deposit), dep (repeat deposit), sale (course, VIP, subscription), qualified (CPA qualified) or rejected (chargeback or reversal: removes the revenue and sends nothing to ad platforms). Later deposits from the same person are recorded as repeat deposits automatically.

Example for Keitaro (S2S postback):

https://joinvoo.com/pb/<your-key>?sub1={sub_id_1}&status={status}&payout={revenue}&txid={tid}

Macro names differ between tools; check your network’s postback help page. Joinvoo also accepts subid, aff_sub or tg_id for the Telegram ID; event or goal for the status; value or amount for the payout.

To optimise for depositors, pick Purchase (Meta), CompletePayment (TikTok) or PURCHASE (Snapchat) as the conversion event in your ad set once deposits start coming in.

9For channels · 2 minutes

Join-request mode: deposits from channels

In a normal channel, Joinvoo knows who joined but can’t give your affiliate program a way to recognise that person. Join-request mode fixes that, so deposits from channel members can be matched too.

  1. In Channels, open your channel and switch Join mode to Join requests. Your bot needs the Invite users via link admin right (it already has it).
  2. Write a short welcome message, a button label and your offer link. Put {tg_id} where your program expects the sub ID, for example https://partner.example.com/?sub1={tg_id}.
  3. That’s it. Each click gets its own join-request link. When someone taps Request to join, the bot approves them instantly, records the join against the click, and sends them your welcome message with their personal link.
  4. When they register or deposit through that link, your program sends the postback with their Telegram ID, and Joinvoo sends Purchase (Meta), CompletePayment (TikTok) or PURCHASE (Snapchat) with the value.
People don’t wait: approval happens in about a second. The welcome message is sent once, by your own bot, so it comes from your brand.

Joinvoo is prepaid with Joinvoo Credits. 1 credit = $0.01. You buy credits and tracking uses them, so there are no surprise bills.

Basic: 3,000 credits a monthThat’s $30. Starts once your 500 free joins are used, charged on the first ad click of each month. Includes 2,000 tracked joins. Pro is 9,900 credits ($99) with 5,000 joins.

Buy credits from Billing (or tap your balance at the top of the dashboard) with card, bank transfer or mobile money, or with crypto. Card payments show up straight away; crypto and bank transfers appear once confirmed.

Bonus credits: bigger single purchases get extra credits, for example +5% from $100, +10% from $500, +15% from $1,000 and +20% from $5,000 (current offer; the live tiers are shown on the Buy credits page). Promo codes can add more.

Ranks: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond, based on what you’ve paid in total. Higher ranks unlock perks such as a discount on tracked joins. Your rank and what you need for the next one are on the Billing page.

Credits are for tracking. They can’t be withdrawn or refunded (see the Refund Policy). Only referral earnings can be withdrawn. If your credits run out, your ads keep working: people still get into your channel, but tracking pauses until you buy more.

16Smart links · 1 minute

Channel banned? Use a backup channel

If a channel gets banned or restricted, you don’t need to edit or re-submit a single ad. Point its tracking link at another channel you own and every ad keeps sending people there.

  1. Connect your backup channel first (step 4), with the same bot or another one. On Basic you can track up to 3 channels: remove one you no longer use, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited.
  2. Open the old channel in Channels and set Send visitors to → your backup channel.
  3. Done. New clicks get invite links for the backup channel, joins are counted there, and its pixel settings are used.
Keep a backup channel ready before you scale. Switching takes one tap and no new ad review.

Fix a problem

My channel didn’t appear after I added the bot

Make sure you added the bot as an admin, not just a member, and that Invite users via link is on. Then run setup again from the Help tab, and on the channel step tap The bot was already an admin?. Pick your bot and type your channel’s @username (or its ID for a private channel).

The channel says “Needs permission”

Someone removed the bot’s admin rights or switched off Invite users via link. In Telegram: open your channel → Administrators → your bot → switch it back on. Joinvoo picks it up by itself.

Most of my joins show as “Organic”

Those people joined without going through your tracking link: from search (public channels), a link someone shared, or an old invite link in your ad. Check that every ad uses the Joinvoo tracking link, and make the channel private if you can.

My ad platform shows fewer events than Joinvoo

That’s normal. Meta, TikTok and Snapchat only count events they can match to one of their users, and they remove duplicates. Events can also take up to 20 minutes to appear. If a join shows failed, tap it to see the platform’s reason.

“Invalid OAuth access token” or “token expired”

Generate a new access token in your ad platform (for Meta: Events Manager → Settings → Conversions API) and paste it on the Channels page. Joins that failed are retried for a while, so recent ones still get sent.

A deposit postback isn’t matched

Joinvoo needs the person’s Telegram ID in sub1 (or subid, aff_sub, tg_id). Check that your bot adds it to the offer link and that your network sends it back in the postback. Placeholders that weren’t filled in, like {sub1}, are ignored.

My bot stopped doing its other job

A bot can send its messages to one place only. If it was used for something else, either make a new bot for Joinvoo, or (for bot-subscriber tracking) paste your bot’s server address under My bot already runs on its own server so Joinvoo passes everything on.

“Invite links ready” is running low

Telegram limits how fast one bot can make links. For big campaigns, add a second or third bot to the same channel (make each one admin). Joinvoo uses all of them. If links ever run out, visitors still get in through a backup link; only those joins aren’t matched.

Tracking is paused

Your credits ran out, or the month’s plan (3,000 credits) couldn’t be charged. Buy credits from Billing and tracking starts again straight away. Your links kept working the whole time.

Is this allowed by Telegram?

Yes. Joinvoo only uses Telegram’s official Bot API, the same invite-link features any admin can use. Your bot never posts in your channel.

What do you store about the people who join?

Their Telegram ID, name, username and language, plus the ad click details (time, campaign, browser and IP). Ad platforms receive a scrambled (hashed) Telegram ID, never the raw one. See our privacy policy.
