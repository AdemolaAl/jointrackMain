# Vertical: Dating and Social Apps
> Use when: someone runs or plans traffic for dating sites/apps or social discovery products: SOI vs DOI, mainstream vs adult, funnels via Telegram, angles, policy and metrics.

Dating is a classic affiliate vertical with simple conversion events (sign-ups) and paid conversions later. On Meta, TikTok and Snapchat only mainstream dating is possible, and usually only with restrictions or prior permission. Adult dating and sexual content are not allowed on these platforms. Joe does not help with adult offers on these platforms or with fake profiles.

---

## 1. Offer models

| Model | Paid on | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SOI (single opt-in) | Registration (email/phone/social login) | Easier conversion, lower payout, more quality checks |
| DOI (double opt-in) | Registration + confirmation (email click, phone verification) | Harder conversion, higher payout, better quality |
| CPS / first purchase | First payment (subscription, credits) | Highest payout, lowest volume |
| RevShare | Share of subscription revenue | Long tail; depends on retention |
| CPI | App install (sometimes with an in-app event) | For app-based dating products |

Joinvoo statuses: `reg` for SOI, `lead` or `qualified` for DOI (depending on what the program reports), `sale` or `ftd` for first payment, `dep` for renewals/repeat purchases.

### Quality rules
- Programs reject fake, duplicate, bot or incentivised registrations.
- Age-verified adults only (18+; higher where required).
- Some programs require profile completion or a minimum activity level.
- Geo rules: registrations from outside the deal's countries are not paid.

## 2. Mainstream vs adult

| | Mainstream dating | Adult / casual sex |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Relationship-focused or general dating, social discovery, friendship apps | Sexual content, hook-up focus, explicit imagery |
| Meta / TikTok / Snapchat | Restricted: Meta requires prior written permission for dating ads and has creative rules; TikTok and Snapchat allow some dating with restrictions in some markets. Check current policies. | Not allowed. Sexual content, adult dating and suggestive imagery are prohibited. |
| Telegram | Allowed within Telegram's terms | Telegram restricts sexual content in many contexts and app stores add further limits |

Compliant alternative for adult offers: do not run them on these platforms. Use mainstream products for social ads.

---

## 3. Policy essentials for dating ads (check current policies)

- **Prior permission** (Meta): dating ads require approval; ads must follow Meta's dating requirements.
- **Age**: target 18+ only; some platforms require higher ages in some markets.
- **No sexual or suggestive content**: no nudity, sexual poses, focus on body parts, sexual language or innuendo.
- **No fake profiles or "someone is waiting for you" bait** suggesting a specific real person wants to meet the viewer.
- **No personal attributes**: avoid "Single and lonely?" or anything implying the viewer's relationship status, sexual orientation or other personal traits.
- **Images**: people should be fully clothed and the focus on connection, not attractiveness ratings.
- **Honesty**: the product promoted in the ad must be the product the user lands on.

## 4. Funnels via Telegram

Dating funnels on Telegram are less direct than in trading or gambling, but they can work:

1. **Ad → bot quiz → offer**: "Find your dating style in 5 questions" → result → personalised recommendation with tracked link (Telegram ID in sub1). Track bot Starts in Joinvoo, and `reg` / `qualified` postbacks from the program.
2. **Ad → channel with dating advice → offer**: content on profiles, first messages, safety tips; offer link in welcome message and posts.
3. **Ad → local community group** (social discovery, events, friendship): community value first, partner app as a tool.

Content that helps conversion:
- Profile and photo tips.
- Conversation starters.
- Safety tips (meet in public places, don't send money to people you haven't met, recognise romance scams).
- Honest app reviews (if you are an affiliate, disclose it).

Never:
- Create fake profiles or fake chats in the channel or bot to lure users.
- Use romance-scam-like scripts.
- Use AI-generated "girls/boys near you" images presented as real people.

## 5. Angles

### Compliant and effective
- Shared interests: "Meet people who love hiking/music/football".
- Local events and community.
- Product features: verified profiles, safety features, video chat.
- Life stage: "Dating after 40" style framing is risky for personal-attribute policies; prefer product framing like "A dating app designed for people looking for serious relationships".
- Success stories only if real, consented and representative.

### Avoid
- Sexual innuendo, provocative imagery.
- "Hot singles in your area".
- Implying the viewer is lonely, divorced or unattractive.
- Fake urgency ("She's online now, reply in 5 minutes").

## 6. Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Click→Start / join | Starts or joins ÷ clicks | Hook match with bot/channel |
| Start→reg | registrations ÷ Starts/joins | Bot flow quality, offer relevance |
| SOI→DOI | DOI ÷ SOI | Real emails/phones, real intent |
| Reg→paid | first payments ÷ registrations | Product fit, audience quality |
| Cost per SOI / DOI / sale | spend ÷ event | Compare with payout |

Targets:
- Break-even cost per registration = payout per registration × acceptance rate (share the program accepts).
- For CPS/RevShare: break-even = expected revenue per paying user × reg→paid rate, per registration.
- Target = break-even × (1 − margin).

Diagnosis:
- **High SOI, low DOI**: users enter fake emails/numbers (bait angle or incentive), or the confirmation step is broken. Test the flow yourself.
- **Good DOI, low paid**: audience not ready to pay; age or geo mismatch; product mismatch.
- **Program rejects many leads**: bot traffic or incentivised sources; check Joinvoo suspect clicks and filtered joins.

---

## 7. Geos and demographics
- Payouts and paying behaviour differ widely by country; tier 1 pays more but costs more.
- Age bands matter: younger users register more but often pay less; older users can be higher value. Respect platform age restrictions and avoid age-based personal attribute claims in copy.
- Local language creatives and local cultural norms around dating are essential; what is acceptable messaging varies a lot.

---

## 8. Common mistakes
1. Running adult or suggestive creatives on Meta, TikTok or Snapchat.
2. Running dating ads on Meta without permission.
3. Fake profiles or fake chat screenshots.
4. Judging on SOI volume while the program pays on DOI or sales.
5. Not passing the Telegram ID to the offer link.
6. Ignoring safety messaging; romance scam reports damage channels.

## 9. Launch plan and testing matrix

### First week
1. Confirm permission/eligibility for dating ads on the platform and that the offer accepts the geo.
2. Build the bot flow: Start → 3–5 light questions → recommendation → tracked offer link (sub1 = Telegram ID) → safety tips.
3. Launch 3 angles × 2–3 hooks each, broad 18+ targeting in one geo, optimise for bot Start or join.
4. Add `reg` and DOI/`qualified` postbacks so Joinvoo shows cost per SOI and DOI per ad.
5. After 3–5 days, rank ads by cost per DOI (or per paying user if there is volume), not by cost per Start.

### Angle matrix to test
| Angle | Example hook (compliant) |
|---|---|
| Shared interests | "Meet people who are into the same music as you" |
| Safety and verification | "Verified profiles and video chat before you meet" |
| Intent | "An app for people looking for something serious" |
| Local community | "Events and meetups in your city this month" |
| Ease | "Set up your profile in 2 minutes" |

### Joe's quick answers
- "Can I use attractive models in the creative?" → Clothed, natural images focused on connection and activities are fine; suggestive poses, body focus or "hot singles" framing will be rejected.
- "Program says my leads are low quality." → Compare SOI→DOI by ad in Joinvoo; drop ads with bait hooks; check suspect clicks; remove any incentive messaging.
- "Should I go after older audiences?" → Test age bands as separate ad sets if the platform allows, judge on cost per paying user, and keep copy free of age or relationship-status callouts.

### Retention and LTV
- For RevShare or subscription deals, value comes from users who keep paying. Content in the channel about profile tips and safe dating keeps users engaged with the product.
- Track `dep` (renewal) postbacks if the program reports them, and shift budget toward campaigns whose users renew.
