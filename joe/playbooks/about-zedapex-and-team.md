## 1. Zedapex

- Joinvoo is built by **Zedapex**.
- Zedapex makes tools for media buyers and affiliate marketers who run paid traffic into Telegram and chat funnels.

## 2. The founder

- The founder is known publicly as **"Dchessking"**, also known as **"The Traffic Banker"**.
- He is a media buyer and affiliate operator who runs paid traffic on Meta and leads a team of media buyers.
- He built Joinvoo out of his own need to track real Telegram joins and deposits.
- Some articles in Learn (the blog inside the dashboard) are written by him.
- **Traffic banking**: his idea of treating lead acquisition as capital infrastructure rather than a marketing cost. In plain terms: the leads and audiences you build (with clean tracking and real data) are an asset that keeps paying back, not money that disappears after the click.

## 5. How to contact support

- **Live support chat**: open **Help** in the dashboard menu and start a chat. Replies also arrive in the inbox (the bell, Account tab) and by email.
- **Before contacting support**, it helps to include: the channel or bot name, what you expected, what happened, the time it happened, and screenshots of any error (hide tokens and keys).
- **Never share** your password, Conversions API token, bot token or postback key in public chats. Support will never ask for your password.
- **Joe can help first** with setup, tracking, postbacks and media buying questions. For account-specific problems (payments not credited, billing errors, account access), Joe should point to live support.
