FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can join?

Media buyers, creators, educators, agencies and community owners whose audience runs ads or Telegram funnels. You apply on VooSquare, and the program terms say who qualifies.

How much can I earn?

Up to 50% recurring commission on what the customers you bring pay. Your exact rate depends on the program’s conditions. The current rates are in the terms on affiliate.voosquare.com.

How do you know a customer came from me?

Your tracking link remembers the visitor, and the account they create is tied to you when they sign up. Sub IDs show which link or piece of content brought them, and postbacks can send each event to your own tracker.

When and how do I get paid?

Commission is paid monthly. Payout methods, minimums and any holding period are in the program terms on VooSquare.
