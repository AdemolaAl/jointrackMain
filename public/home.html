FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an FTD postback, and do I need one?

An FTD (first-time deposit) postback is a small web request your broker, casino, affiliate network, CRM or bot sends when someone registers or deposits. Your account has its own postback URL, for example https://joinvoo.com/pb/<key>?sub1={telegram_id}&status=ftd&payout={payout}&txid={transaction_id}.

Joinvoo matches it to the person who joined and sends Purchase (with value) to Meta, CompletePayment to TikTok and PURCHASE to Snapchat. You don’t need it to track joins, but it lets your ads optimise for depositors. It works with Keitaro and Binom-style postbacks, and you can also mark someone as deposited by hand.

Does it work with Snapchat ads?

Yes. Paste your Snap Pixel ID and a Conversions API token, send a test, and use your tracking link as the ad’s web URL. Joins go to Snapchat as SUBSCRIBE and deposits as PURCHASE with value. Meta and TikTok work the same way, and you can connect all three at once.

Channels only, or groups and bots too?

All three. Track people who join a channel or group, or who press Start on your bot. Bots can send a welcome message and button when someone starts them.

What’s the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic ($30 a month) tracks joins and sends them to Meta, TikTok and Snapchat. Pro ($99 a month) adds deposit and sale tracking, cost per FTD and ROAS dashboards, affiliate integrations and Joe’s insights. Every account gets a free deposit trial: 7 days or 20 FTDs, whichever comes first. After the trial on Basic, deposits are still sent to your ad platforms so your ads keep learning; you just can’t see which ads drove them until you upgrade.

My channel got banned. Do I need to change my ads?

No. Open the channel in Joinvoo and point its tracking link at your backup channel. Every ad keeps running and new joins are counted on the backup channel, with no ad edits and no new review.

Who is Joe?

Joe is your built-in media-buying assistant. He shows quick insights on your dashboard (tracking health, best and worst campaigns, cost per FTD changes, quiet channels) and answers questions about your own numbers. He never invents data: if something isn’t tracked, he says so.

What are Joinvoo Credits?

Joinvoo is prepaid with credits: 1 credit = $0.01. The Basic plan is 3,000 credits ($30) a month including 2,000 tracked joins; Pro is 9,900 credits ($99) a month including 5,000. Extra joins cost 2 credits each. Bigger top-ups earn bonus credits. Credits are for tracking; they can’t be withdrawn or refunded. Only referral earnings can be withdrawn.

What happens when my credits run out?

Your ads keep working. Visitors still get into your channel. Joinvoo just pauses tracking and stops sending joins to your ad platforms until you buy more credits. You’ll see a banner in your dashboard when it happens.

Is this allowed by Telegram?

Yes. Joinvoo only uses Telegram’s official Bot API: the same invite-link features any channel admin can use. Your bot never posts in your channel.

What do you store about the people who join?

Their Telegram ID, name, username and language, plus the ad click details (time, campaign, browser and IP). Ad platforms receive a hashed (scrambled, one-way) Telegram ID, never the raw one. Read the privacy policy.

Will my ads pass review?

If a platform’s review is strict, switch on the Join page. Visitors see a clean page with a Join button instead of being sent straight to Telegram.

Can it handle a lot of traffic?

Yes. Add several bots to one channel and Joinvoo uses all of them, handling 300k+ clicks a day. Invite links are made ahead of time, and if they ever run out, visitors still get in through a backup link.

How do I pay?

Buy credits by card, bank transfer or mobile money through Paystack or Flutterwave, or with crypto. New accounts get their first 500 tracked joins free after confirming their email, so you don’t need to buy anything to start.
